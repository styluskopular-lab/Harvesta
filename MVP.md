# Harvesta Uganda MVP

## MVP promise

The MVP helps Ugandan farmers and families complete the core farm-to-table journey:

- Create a buyer, farmer, driver or shop-manager account with Gmail or phone login.
- Record an account location using district and GPS coordinates.
- Browse Uganda-only fresh produce and locally made products.
- Place a real UGX order with a delivery address.
- Track orders and delivery vehicles.
- View seven-day weather forecasts for farm planning.
- Find veterinary professionals and request livestock or animal-care visits.
- Farmers can register a farm, list products and manage harvest inventory.
- Users can switch between light and dark backgrounds.

## MVP exclusions

The first release does not claim live payment collection, SMS verification, Google OAuth, real-time dispatch, or veterinary diagnosis. These integrations require licensed providers and production credentials.
